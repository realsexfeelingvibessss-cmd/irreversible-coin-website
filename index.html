About IRC

The Irreversible Coin is a proposed Solana token project focused on a clearly defined supply and publicly verifiable allocations.

Fixed supply plan

A planned maximum supply of 3.33 billion IRC, with no future minting intended.

Transparent allocations

Each allocation is intended to use a separately identified wallet or on-chain program.

Solana ecosystem

Designed for the Solana network. Network deployment and contract details are still to be confirmed.
